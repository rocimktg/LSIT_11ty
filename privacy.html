PRIVACY & WEBSITE USE POLICY

How Lone Star Institute of Taekwondo collects, uses, and protects your information online.

Effective Date: 12/19/25

Lone Star Institute of Taekwondo respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected, as well as the basic terms governing use of this website.


INFORMATION WE COLLECT

  • Submit a contact or inquiry form
  • Sign up for a free class or trial
  • Subscribe to email or text communications

Information collected may include your name, email address, phone number, and basic details related to your inquiry or interest in our programs.


HOW WE USE INFORMATION

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Provide information about classes, schedules, and programs
  • Schedule trials or introductory classes
  • Improve our services and website experience

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.


COOKIES AND ANALYTICS

This website may use cookies or analytics tools to collect general usage data such as page visits, traffic sources, and device types. This information is used solely to understand website performance and improve usability.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings if desired.


DATA SECURITY

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect your personal information. While we take steps to safeguard data, no method of online transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.


THIRD PARTY SERVICES

We may use third party services such as website hosting, analytics tools, email platforms, or scheduling software to support normal business operations. These providers operate under their own privacy policies.


CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

This website is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without parental or guardian consent.


WEBSITE USE

This website is provided for informational purposes only. Content may include class descriptions, schedules, pricing, promotions, photos, videos, and general information about Lone Star Institute of Taekwondo.

You agree not to use this website for any unlawful purpose or in a way that could harm the school, its instructors, students, or reputation.

All content on this website, including text, images, videos, logos, and branding, is the property of Lone Star Institute of Taekwondo unless otherwise noted. Content may not be copied, reproduced, or distributed without written permission.


LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Lone Star Institute of Taekwondo is not responsible for damages arising from the use or inability to use this website, including indirect or incidental damages.


POLICY UPDATES

This policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.


CONTACT

If you have questions about this policy or need assistance accessing website content, please contact us using the information provided on this website.
